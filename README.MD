🐾 Penpot Mockup – Kitten Photo Shop
Welcome to the initial mockup of "mignon chaton", an online store dedicated to selling the most adorable kitten photos 🐱📸

🎯 Project Goal
The goal is to design a warm, simple, and delightful user experience that makes browsing and purchasing kitten photos both fun and easy. The site targets cat lovers, collectors, and anyone looking for high-quality, heart-melting images.

🖼️ What's Included in the Mockup
This first version of the mockup includes the following screens:

Home – A welcoming landing page with a featured photo gallery and categories.

Photo Page – Detailed view of a selected photo with purchase options (size, format, license).

Cart – A summary of selected photos before checkout.

Checkout – A clear and user-friendly form to complete the purchase.

Confirmation – A thank-you message with order details.

💡 The overall tone is soft, joyful, and emotionally engaging — just like the kittens it showcases!

🎨 Design Notes
Visual Style: Minimal, image-focused layout that gives full attention to the photos.

Color Palette: Soft pastel tones to convey calmness and cuteness.

Typography: Rounded and friendly fonts for a welcoming look.

UX Approach: Mobile-first, intuitive navigation, with a smooth shopping flow.
